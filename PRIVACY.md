# HealthSense AI — Privacy Policy

HealthSense AI is a personal research project that answers general health
questions over WhatsApp. It is not a medical service and does not provide
diagnosis or treatment.

## What we process
- The text of messages you send to our WhatsApp number, used only to
  generate a reply.
- Conversation history is stored under a pseudonymous identifier
  (a one-way hash), not under your phone number.

## What we do not do
- We do not sell or share your data with third parties.
- We do not use your messages for advertising.
- We do not store your phone number alongside your health questions.

## Data processors
Messages are transmitted via Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform and
processed with Anthropic's Claude API to generate replies.
